Revenue trend

Monthly revenue across the full 4.5-year history. 2024 covers January–July only (partial year).

Finding: revenue is stable-to-growing through 2023, then drops sharply in 2024 — the partial year (Jan–Jul only) plus a near-total collapse from March onward, well beyond what a partial year explains.

Revenue by category

Share of total revenue by product category.

Finding: electronics account for 89.7% of all revenue — extreme category concentration. The biggest risk to this business is operational (returns, delays), not demand diversity.

Payment health

Distribution of payment outcomes across all 21,629 payments.

Finding: 84.6% of payments succeed, but 13.1% are refunded and 2.3% fail — the refund share mirrors the 13.4% return rate, confirming returns are the primary revenue leak.
